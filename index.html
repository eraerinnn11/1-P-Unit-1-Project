The Saxophone

There are four main types of the Saxophone

  • Soprano
  • Alto
  • Tenor
  • Baritone
(in order of size)

About The Saxophone

The Saxophone is a single reed instrument that is usually made out of brass. The sound is made as the reed
vibrates against the mouthpiece and produce a soundwave that is to be manipulated as you press buttons to
either open or close holes, which will alter the pitch, creating a specific note. The saxophone has 22 to 23 buttons,
depending on, age and make of the instrument. To tell the difference between the types, other than looking at the size,
you can look at the necks of the instrument. A Soprano's is straight, an Alto's is at an ~130 degree angle, a Tenor's is
like an alto, but it has a slight curve in it, and a Baritone's is curved.
